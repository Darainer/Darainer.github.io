03 / Datasets, labelling pipelines & AI flywheels

Ship once. Stand still.
Build the data flywheel.

The first release is the starting line. Without a way to capture failures, improve datasets and feed learning back into the product, progress stalls.

I help companies connect real-world use to better data, reliable labelling, stronger models and focused product updates. Smaller, validated improvements build momentum with every cycle.

One connected improvement loop

01 / ACQUIRE & CURATE

Get the
right datasets.

Source and capture representative data around customer use cases, coverage gaps and observed failures. Preserve the context and provenance needed to use it well.

02 / LABEL & CHECK

Make labelling
a reliable pipeline.

Define the annotation task, guidelines and quality checks. Turn incoming data into consistent, versioned training and evaluation datasets.

03 / TRAIN & VALIDATE

Measure the
next improvement.

Train against a clear hypothesis. Evaluate on held-out data and relevant scenarios, checking regressions and the behaviour of the integrated system.

04 / RELEASE & LEARN

Improve the product.
Learn from its use.

Release focused changes against explicit acceptance criteria. Use operational feedback to identify the next data gap and product improvement.
